Push-ups

The counter that only counts what it can see.

Put the phone on the floor in front of you. The front camera watches your form, scores the reps it can verify, and stays quiet for the ones it can’t. No wearable, no manual tally, no arguing with a number you didn’t earn.

Video and pose landmarks never leave your device. Read the policy

How it works

Pose estimation runs on the phone at 15 frames a second. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is recorded.

It hears a rep, you hear a ding

Reach the depth threshold and it counts. Come up short and it still counts, at a discount — a half rep is honest work, not a failure.

Measured against you

Depth is scaled to your own shoulder width, so the threshold means the same thing on an incline, on your knees, or flat on the floor.

Showing up is the metric

A single counted rep holds your streak. A light day is a choice the app respects rather than a lapse it punishes.

Friends, never a leaderboard

Challenges are scored in share of your own daily budget, so a beginner and an athlete compete on effort instead of capacity.

What it doesn’t do

It’s a rep verifier, not a form coach.

Worth saying plainly, because the opposite claim is the easy one to make and it falls apart the first time someone sags through fifty reps and scores perfectly.

  • It checks depth and tempo. It does not judge whether your hips are level.
  • It counts push-ups. One exercise, done properly, rather than a library.
  • There is no global leaderboard, and there is not going to be one.
