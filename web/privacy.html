Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 August 2026

Video and pose landmarks never leave your device; workout results and your friends list do.

That sentence is the whole policy in one line. The rest of this page explains exactly what sits on each side of it, because an app that asks you to point a camera at yourself on your bedroom floor owes you a precise answer rather than a reassuring one.

What never leaves your device

The camera feed is processed entirely on your phone. Pose estimation runs locally, frame by frame, while you are in a set.

  • Video. No frame of the camera feed is uploaded, transmitted, or written to storage. It is not recorded even temporarily.
  • Pose landmarks. The coordinates describing where your body is in a frame exist in memory for the duration of a rep and are then discarded. They are never uploaded and never written to disk.

There is no setting that turns this off, no “help us improve” toggle that opts you into uploading footage, and no debug mode that ships in the App Store build and behaves differently. The camera is the only sensor the app uses, and what it sees stays on the phone.

What does leave your device

Your results sync to our server so that they survive losing your phone and so that friend challenges work. This is real data about you, held on a server we operate, and it is worth naming item by item.

What Why it leaves the device
Identity — your account, sign-in details and display name So your history is yours across reinstalls, and so friends can find you.
Your friends list — the friend graph Challenges are friends-only, so the connections have to be stored. We never upload or scan your phone’s contacts to build it.
XP ledger — what you earned, when Streaks, levels and challenge standings are computed from it.
Rep Logs — per-rep metrics: depth ratio, tempo, variant, full or half, and detector confidence A small derived record per rep. Enough to recompute whether a rep should have counted — which is what lets us investigate a miscount — with no reconstructable body, room, or person in it.

A Rep Log is a handful of numbers. It is not an image, not a landmark stream, and nothing in it can be reassembled into a picture of you or of the room you were in.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, completed sessions are written to HealthKit as workouts so that your push-ups sit alongside the rest of your training history and your rings close. The permission is write-only: the app does not read anything from HealthKit, and it will work normally if you decline.

Health data carries obligations we take as absolute commitments:

  • Data written to or read from HealthKit is never used for advertising or any use-based data mining, by us or by anyone else.
  • It is never sold to data brokers, advertising platforms, or information resellers. We do not sell your personal data at all.
  • It is used only to give you your own workout history inside the app and in Apple Health.

Who else sees your data

Your friends see what you would expect them to see: your display name and your standing in challenges you are both part of, scored as a share of each person’s own daily budget. They do not see your rep logs.

We use Supabase to host the database and authentication, and Vercel to host this website. They process data on our behalf as infrastructure providers and do not have the right to use it for their own purposes. Beyond that, we do not share, rent, or sell your data with anyone, and there are no advertising or analytics SDKs collecting your behaviour in the app.

Your control

  • Deletion. You can delete your account from within the app. That removes your identity, your friend graph, your XP ledger and your rep logs from our server.
  • Export. You can request a copy of what we hold about you by emailing us.
  • Apple Health. Revoke the write permission any time in the Health app. Data already written there is yours and is managed by Apple Health, not by us.
  • Camera. Revoke camera access in iOS Settings. Rep counting is the only thing that uses it, so the counter stops working, but nothing else changes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — particularly the sentence at the top of this page — we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to export or delete your data: privacy@pushups.app.
